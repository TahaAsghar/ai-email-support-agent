# Coffee Grinders

## Models and finishes
We sell four electric grinder models, each in four finishes: matte black, brushed steel, alpine white, and forest green.

- GR-S1: single-dose grinder, 64 mm flat burrs.

- GR-S3: hopper-fed grinder, 64 mm flat burrs, timer display.

- GR-C4: commercial-grade flat burr grinder, 83 mm burrs.

- GR-C9: high-speed conical burr grinder, 71 mm burrs.
The model code is printed on the bottom label and on the user manual.

## Parts lists

- GR-S1: 1 motor base, 2 bean hopper, 3 bellows lid, 4 dosing cup, 5 power cord, 6 burr carrier.

- GR-S3: 1 motor base, 2 large hopper, 3 hopper lid, 4 ground coffee bin, 5 power cord, 6 burr carrier, 7 display module.

- GR-C4: 1 heavy motor base, 2 reinforced hopper, 3 portafilter fork, 4 touch panel, 5 power cord, 6 83mm burr set, 7 drip tray.

- GR-C9: 1 heavy motor base, 2 conical hopper, 3 portafilter cradle, 4 cooling fan unit, 5 power cord, 6 conical burr set, 7 drip tray.
Hardware kits: HK-G for the S models, HK-GC for the C models. A kit holds all replacement set screws, Allen keys, and rubber feet.

## Assembly and setup tips
- Place the grinder on a stable, dry countertop away from splash zones.

- Burr alignment: the alignment marker on the outer burr carrier aligns with the index dot on the housing. Turn clockwise until hand-tight.

- The bean hopper seats firmly with a twist-lock motion until it clicks into the safety microswitch.

- Heavy units (GR-C4, GR-C9) must be kept on a flat, level surface and never lifted by the hopper.

- Initial calibration takes 15 to 30 minutes. Running a small test batch of cleaning beans is recommended before first use.

## Problems and what happens next
A colleague handles every shipment. Never promise one in a reply.

- Missing screws or hardware: we ship the hardware kit for the model.

- 1 or 2 damaged exterior parts: we ship those parts by part number.

- 3 or more damaged parts, or a damaged motor housing: we send a full replacement.

- Wrong finish delivered: we exchange it for the right finish.
To arrange anything, we need the order number, the model, and the part numbers of damaged parts. A photo helps.
