# Returns and Waranty

## Returns
All orders go through Amazon, so returns do too. The customer starts a return in Your Orders on Amazon within 30 days of delivery. Amazon sends the return label and handles the refund.
We cannot start, speed up, or approve a return or refund from our side. A colleague answers every refund question.

## Warranty
- Espresso machines: 2 years on the boiler, pump, and group head. 1 year on the portafilter, steam wand, and baskets.

- Grinders: 2 years on the motor and housing. 1 year on the burrs and hopper.
The warranty covers manufacturing faults. It does not cover damage from misuse, such as scale buildup from untreated tap water or foreign objects in the burrs.

## Other products
For any other BaristaCraft product, the same rules apply: returns through Amazon within 30 days, and a 2-year warranty on manufacturing faults.
