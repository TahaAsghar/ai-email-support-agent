## Water pressure and stability
Operate between 9 to 12 PSI brew pressure. Below 9 PSI the shot pulls too weak.
Losing 1 to 2 PSI pressure overnight during cooling is normal. That is not a leak.

## Valve check for slow pressure loss
Most pressure loss comes from a loose valve core, not from the machine boiler. Ask the customer to do this first:
1. Warm up the machine to operating temperature.
2. Spray soapy water on the steam valve joint. Bubbles mean steam or air escapes there.
3. Put the valve wrench on the valve core and turn it a quarter turn clockwise.
4. Spray again, then check the pressure after 24 hours.
If the machine still loses more than 2 PSI in 24 hours, ask for the order number, the machine model, and how many days ago the machine arrived.

## Problems and what happens next
A colleague handles every shipment. Never promise one in a reply.
- Missing accessory: we ship the missing part. No return needed.
- Slow pressure loss: valve check first.
- Still leaking after the valve check, within 30 days of delivery: we send a new machine.
- Still leaking after the valve check, more than 30 days after delivery: we send a seal patch kit.
- Split boiler seam: always goes to a colleague.
To arrange anything, we need the order number, the machine model, and a short description of the problem.
